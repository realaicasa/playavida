# PlayaVida: Strategic Vision & Roadmap

## Monetization Strategy
- **Sponsors:** 1,200 MXN/mo (billed 3,600 MXN per quarter). Placement: Top rail only.
- **Business Pro:** 800 MXN/mo (billed 2,400 MXN per quarter). Features: Dashboard, Inbox, Loyalty management, Analytics.
- **Rules:** All subscriptions are recurring every 3 months. No pro features or sponsor placements visible without active subscription.
- **Messaging Policy:** No storage of communications beyond initial inquiry. Required login for users. Non-logged-in users see a popup modal explaining the privacy requirement.
- **Loyalty:** All loyalty cards require a Pro Business subscription. Admin can add/remove up to 10 staff members with unique 4-digit PINs.

## Moderation & Admin Control
- **Report System:** Admins see all reports. Capability to purge/block any user/entity/sponsor immediately.
- **Account Labeling:** All test/demo accounts must be clearly labeled "Demo Account".

## Strategy Doc Update

## Future Roadmap: 'Outstanding' Features
- **Hyper-Personalized Engagement:** AI-Powered "Coastal Feed" based on verified user interests.
- **Gamified Exploration:** "Community Quests" and digital badges for local discovery.
- **Business Automation:** Automated bookings, event check-ins, and proactive Pro-tier CRM alerts.
- **Predictive Analytics:** AI-driven traffic and trend forecasting for businesses.
- **Collaborative Itineraries:** Users creating/sharing collections of businesses, events, and groups.
- **Contextual Discovery:** Geolocation-aware notifications for loyalty milestones or nearby businesses.
