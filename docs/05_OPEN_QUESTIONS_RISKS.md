# Open Questions & Risks

- Brand: "PlayaVida" embeds a place; for licensing, decide master brand + per-city sub-brand (e.g. "[Brand] [City]"). Check trademark availability and register early (Mexico: IMPI). Verify with a lawyer.
- Pay-to-push vs "no ads, no algorithms" promise: reword to "no hidden ads; sponsored items always labelled", or stay true to the promise by capping and labelling.
- Privacy/consent: Mexico's data protection law was reformed in 2025; confirm current obligations (consent, privacy notice, ARCO rights) with a local lawyer. GDPR-style rules may apply to EU nomads.
- Web push on iOS only works for installed PWAs (iOS 16.4+): verify current behaviour; email digest is the reliable fallback.
- Franchise rules: Mexico has franchise disclosure requirements; verify before selling licences.
- Tenant data isolation: pick separate-base vs shared-base before building more.
- Teable limits (rate limits, webhooks, scale): test before relying on it for audience filtering.
- Cold start: pilot success metric needed (e.g. X verified businesses, Y weekly active members) before cloning.
