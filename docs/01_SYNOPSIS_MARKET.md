# Synopsis & Target Market

## Core concept (generic, no location names)
A community-powered digital ecosystem that connects residents, nomads and local entrepreneurs: not a directory but a platform that turns local discovery into real-world community impact (activities, groups, verified providers, events, volunteering).

## Value propositions
- Residents/expats: authentic local activities, verified providers, like-minded neighbours, without noisy social feeds.
- Business owners: owned mini-webpage, built-in loyalty program, direct community access, no algorithm dependence.
- Community: circular local economy, ecological impact, stronger bonds.

## Target segments (v1, pilot market)
1. Expats & long-term residents (need trusted providers, belonging)
2. Digital nomads (short stays; need co-working, events, social ties)
3. Local small businesses & wellness/experience providers (need cheap, owned visibility)
4. Event organisers & group admins (need audience reach)
5. Sponsors/brands (need targeted local reach)
Pilot market: configured in superadmin (currently Playa del Carmen). NEVER hardcoded.

## Business model
- Free registration
- Pro tiers (Stripe): business profile upgrades, analytics, loyalty
- Paid targeted push/newsletter placements (weekly)
- Licensing/franchise of the platform + management playbook to other regions (long-term)

## Honest risks
Data quality at scale | cold-start network effects vs Big Tech | trust/scam perception | consent & privacy compliance | pay-to-push vs "no ads" promise.
